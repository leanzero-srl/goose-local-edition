Tier weights and critical multiplier floors are unchanged. The developing label remains sb-8.0-rc; standalone verdicts now retain hashes of the scorer, browser probe and oracle for exact provenance.

## Compact planning amendment

The pre-planning task proved too prescriptive for Gemini 3.8 Flash:1.0000 in8m03s. The revised prompt adds one HTTP capability, POST/api/plan, sharing the existing swept geometry. It must find minimum-energy paths on a finite pose lattice, accept any optimal tie, leave state/receipts unchanged, and report unreachable goals. No additional UI, service or documentation deliverable is required. New weights are recorded in each verdict: backend25%, scene30%, interaction15%, planning25%, excellence5%; historical results lacking the planning metadata retain their original formula. This is a new prompt for a fresh run, never a retroactive penalty on the saved baseline.

Independent replay validates every candidate route's lattice adjacency, one-axis moves, swept safety, actual energy and optimality; a claimed optimum alone earns nothing. The live grading scenarios—not only unit fixtures—include a three-edge detour costing13 versus a three-edge climb costing11, a passage requiring rotate/traverse/rotate-back, and a disconnected graph with valid endpoints. Healthy, fewest-edge and fixed-yaw planners were exercised across7seeds; the two mutants fail their respective live checks. The independent refuter reproduced those results. Eight oracle/API tests include exact read-only SQLite state/receipt proof; three grading tests include forged cost, unsafe/teleporting routes and feasible suboptimal routes.
