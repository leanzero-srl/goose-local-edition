Draft, 26 October 2026. Prepare alongside the app; publish only when the release has proved the advertised paths. This is LeanZero's first App Store app, not a claim to have invented mobile agent control. The first release is a free companion; confirm the final pricing and seller identity in App Store Connect.

## Position and discoverability

Audience: people running Goose Swarm on their own Macs who need to see what is happening and send a message from another Mac, iPhone or iPad. Lead with continuity of the original session. Models, tools and jobs keep running on the owner Mac; the mobile device is a controller and never a compute node.

Primary branded query: “Goose Remote”. Descriptive page theme: “remote control for AI agents on iPhone and iPad”. Secondary topics: Goose Swarm sessions, monitor AI jobs, remote model downloads, LeanZero Link. These are positioning candidates, not measured search volumes. The phrase “remote control for the coding agents” appears in [Perch's own documentation](https://perchcode.dev/docs/); that supports category vocabulary, not a claim about Goose compatibility with other agents.

Keep the existing app identity while checking availability. Draft App Store name: `Goose Remote by LeanZero`. Draft subtitle: `Your Mac's AI agents, anywhere`. Both fit 30 characters. Keyword draft: `swarm,assistant,sessions,monitor,models,huggingface,benchmark,developer,mac,ipad`. Recheck legal use of product names and actual locale limits before upload. Avoid competitor names and unsupported compatibility claims.

Draft promotional text: “Follow Goose sessions across your Macs. Read updates, send a nudge and manage remote model downloads from your iPhone or iPad.”

Draft opening description: “Keep your Goose Swarm work moving when you step away from your Mac. Goose Remote brings your connected Macs' sessions into one view. Read the latest activity, send a message and answer supported requests while the original Mac keeps doing the work.”

The next paragraph must state requirements prominently: a compatible Goose Swarm installation, LeanZero Link access, and the owner Mac online. Explain the supported session/job controls and remote download/provider operations only after the integrated run verifies them. Explain multiple controllers and manual sync in plain language. No offline-execution, background-streaming, guaranteed-availability, arbitrary-agent or run-migration claims.

Apple permits names/subtitles up to 30 characters, keywords up to 100 characters, promotional text up to 170, and previews up to 30 seconds. Screenshots should show the real UI; make the first images communicate the main use. Use device footage for previews. [Apple product page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/).
