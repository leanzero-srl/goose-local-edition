# 3.0.96 A slow Gauntlet build publishes with its recording

**A build whose 3D view ran past the time limit can now be published.**

- **What happened:** the Gauntlet probe records the whole graded browser session, then writes the clip's manifest when it finishes. When the probe hit its 480-second limit it was stopped before writing that manifest, so the recording existed but the app could not find it, and leanzero.net refuses a Gauntlet result without its graded clip. GLM-5.3's build scored 0.082 and could not be published.
- **The fix:** after a timeout, the scorer writes the manifest itself from the recording on disk and encodes it exactly as before, so the result publishes with its real graded clip.
- **Nothing else changes:** the task, the vendor and every check are the same.
