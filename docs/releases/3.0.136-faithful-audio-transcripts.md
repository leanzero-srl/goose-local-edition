# 3.0.136 — audio transcripts stay transcripts

A fix release on top of 3.0.135, for the audio and video input it introduced.

## What was wrong in 3.0.135

goose sends each attached clip to the local model on its own first and puts the transcript into the chat turn. In
3.0.135 that transcription request also carried your question. With Gemma 4 E4B that was enough, in two of three
runs, for the model to answer the question inside the last 30-second piece instead of transcribing it ("The Vienna
train leaves on Friday at 10 to fix the kitchen sink"), so the chat answer got a time or a repair wrong.

## The fix

- **The transcription request carries only the instruction and the clip**, never your question, and the
  instruction says not to answer, summarise or reorder anything.
- **Audio is cut where it is quiet.** A clip longer than 30 seconds is still heard in pieces of at most 30 seconds,
  but each piece now ends at the quietest moment of its last 5 seconds, so a word is less likely to be cut in two.
  A 2-minute clip now comes back as five pieces with their time spans.
- An attached audio file whose 16 kHz WAV would be over 50 MB (about 26 minutes) is refused with that reason (not
  exercised in the packaged app for this release).

## Measured in the packaged app

On an M3 Max (64 GB) with mlx-community/gemma-4-e4b-it-8bit: the same 2-minute speech clip, attached once as WAV and
once as MP3, came back as complete transcripts both times (nothing missing or invented against the script read into
the clip; numbers come back as digits and one surname, Brandt, as Brand; about 11.5 seconds each), and both chat answers gave all three code words, the train's 7:15 departure
from platform nine and the kitchen sink. The 60-second video and an image were described correctly as in 3.0.135.
The engine's peak memory over the whole session was 15.5 GB.

## What does not change

Video and image handling, the media lane default for audio-capable models, and everything else in 3.0.135.

## Upgrading

Install over 3.0.135 (or let the app update itself). Settings carry over.
