# 3.0.25 Link reconnects by itself; an idle split engine stays up

LeanZero Link now remembers whether you wanted the mesh on and reconnects at every app launch and goosed restart; Disconnect (new) and Log out keep it off, and a failed reconnect shows its reason with Retry in the Link tab and the tray. Verified after installing on two Macs: both came back "Running · 2 nodes" with no clicks.

A distributed engine is no longer stopped when it sits idle. Since idle worker ranks stopped burning a CPU core, the supervisor's stall check read an idle worker as hung and stopped the engine ~20 s after its last request; liveness now keys off requests in flight. Verified on the installed build: Flash split across both Macs stayed Ready through 3 and then 2 idle minutes and answered after each.

Includes everything in 3.0.23 (placement planner and Measure speed, Make room and GPU-ceiling budgets, a model on a linked Mac alone, Flash images, KV compression, engine fixes); 3.0.24 was not published. Measured on the installed apps at the new budgets: Flash across an M4 Max + M3 Ultra at the full 262,144 context, a 26.6k-token prompt in 56 s, two concurrent requests, memory pressure normal in every one-second sample on both Macs; 21.0 tok/s writing, 527 reading.
