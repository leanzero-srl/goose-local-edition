# 3.0.39 When the Mac serving chat drops, you see it

When chat is served by another Mac over LeanZero Link and that Mac stops answering, whether its Link restarts, its app is quit or relaunched, or it crashes, the chat now says so from the first seconds to the moment it is back. Before this release the bar went blank for about ten seconds and the error arrived pasted into the middle of the answer.

What you see now, measured second by second on two Macs (Work's Mac Studio serving the 27B to a MacBook):

- About two seconds after the words stop, the line under the composer says "No words from Work's Mac Studio for a moment — checking…". If the stream is only slow, it goes away again.
- Then a solid amber bar: "Lost contact with Work's Mac Studio — reconnecting…". It names the Mac, keeps a spinner going, and offers "Load Qwen3.8-27B-Atlassian-Q8-mlx here (30.5 GB)" to move chat to this Mac instead. The line under the composer says "Waiting for Work's Mac Studio to reconnect…". The chip reads "Reconnecting", and the menu-bar tray says "Reconnecting to Work's Mac Studio".
- When the other Mac quits goose on purpose, it tells this Mac first. The bar says "Work's Mac Studio quit goose" about two seconds after the quit, and the answer in progress ends straight away instead of waiting for the connection to time out.
- When that Mac comes back and loads the model again, the bar says "Loading … on Work's Mac Studio — a message waits until it is ready". A message sent while it loads now waits for the model instead of failing.
- The cut-off answer stays as written, followed by a notice: "Work's Mac Studio stopped answering mid-reply — the answer above stops there" (or "quit goose mid-answer"), with a Retry button that resends your message. The technical error sits behind Details.

Also in this release:

- The model chip names the model and the Mac serving it, and shows what it is doing in words: Idle, Reading a prompt, Writing, Loading, Reconnecting.
- While the engine reads a long prompt, the line under the composer says so: "reading your prompt (49k tokens) — 28s so far".
- The busy bar no longer says your message waits behind "another request" while it is your own turn, or while goose checks its own last answer. It appears only when a request is actually waiting.
- A Mac that isn't answering never blocks Stop, Run or a switch to this Mac. goose drops the route here at once and asks the other Mac to free its model when it can.
- LeanZero Link restarts its own network process if it dies. A request in flight when the other Mac's Link drops now ends with a named error in under 20 seconds instead of a two-minute silent wait.
- When the other Mac's app relaunches, this Mac reloads the model there by itself, with no clicks.
- The Engine tab shows the memory of the Mac that serves chat, and the tray title names that Mac.

Known issues: an answer can take two to three minutes to start on the 27B when the engine has to read goose's roughly 49k-token prompt from scratch; when its cache still holds the prompt, it starts in seconds. The cache is too small to keep it reliably, and that's being worked on next. During a Link restart the amber bar can clear and come back once for a few seconds, while the tray already says the Mac is back.
