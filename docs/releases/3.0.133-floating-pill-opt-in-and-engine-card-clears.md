# 3.0.133 — the floating pill is opt-in and stays out of your way, and a stopped engine no longer sticks as Failed

A fix release on top of 3.0.132. It fixes two things people ran into: the floating activity window opened the
full goose window when you clicked its ✕ or dragged it, and the engine card stayed red "Failed" for hours after
something outside goose stopped the engine, while the Engine panel kept saying "Checking".

## The floating pill

- **Off until you turn it on.** The floating pill shows only if you tick "Float a pill when goose is minimized"
  under the engine card in the sidebar, or turn on "Show a floating activity pill when goose is minimized or in
  the background" in Settings › App. Existing installs start with it off.
- **✕ closes it and nothing else.** Clicking ✕ hides the pill at once, the goose window does not come forward,
  and the app you were in stays in front. The pill stays closed until something new starts (a new turn, a
  question, a split, a rank or a route).
- **Dragging moves it, never opens goose.** Drag the pill by its body and it moves and snaps to the nearest
  corner, which it remembers. A press that travels more than a few pixels is never a click, even when it started
  on a button.
- **Only ⤢ opens goose.** The pill has three controls: ⤢ opens goose on the Engine tab or the chat that needs
  you, ▴ shows the full card (− shrinks it back), ✕ closes it.
- **The Mac serving a rank shows the pill too.** When this Mac holds a rank of another Mac's split, the pill
  (if you turned it on) shows that work, even while the split waits between requests.
- The real cause was on macOS: a click on the floating window activated goose and, because that window could
  not take focus itself, macOS handed focus to goose's main window and brought it forward. The floating window
  now takes the click itself.

Checked with real mouse clicks and drags on a Mac (not simulated in the page): ✕ with Finder in front, a drag
to another corner, a press on ⤢ dragged away, ⤢, ▴ and −, and the setting across a restart.

## The engine card after an outside stop

- **A stop from outside goose reads Stopped, not Failed.** When something outside goose ends the engine (a
  SIGTERM or Ctrl-C that no goose path sent), the engine reports it as stopped, the Engine panel says "Stopped
  outside goose" with what ended, and the sidebar card no longer shows a red failure.
- **A real failure can be dismissed.** The failed card has a dismiss button. It comes back only if the engine
  fails again with a different error.
- **The Engine panel no longer hangs on "Checking".** Every shared status read and the placement plan now have a
  time limit, so a status call that never answers turns into the real state with a Retry instead of an endless
  spinner.

## Upgrading

Install over 3.0.132. Your settings carry over. The floating pill starts off, tick it if you want it.
